# DJ Doorbell, YouTube film: three storylines (2026-11-05)

Assumptions for all three: 1920x1080, 30 fps, English titles, music = the
doorbell's own 124 BPM track (the reel's track.wav, extended), real door audio
under the payoff, free throughout. Narration is the open question: none
(titles carry the words, like the reel), or the owner's own voice recorded on
a phone from a script I write (free), or a generated voice (HeyGen, paid).
Footage: videos/djdoorbell-reel-a/assets (28 clips, 21 photos), the finished
video of 2026-11-05 (IMG_7157), the two inside-box photos, CAD renders, the
phone page.

## A. "You play it, you don't press it"  (RECOMMENDED: the one a stranger follows and remembers)
Device: the result first, then the object is the explanation. Form: chapters
cut on the beat, one diagram that grows over the real footage.
Length 80-100 s. The viewer sees a hand play the door in the first second and
hears the beat start on the first press. Then the camera pulls back: what was in
the wall (two wires, 8 V), what that forced (12 V on the pair, radio across the
door), and the one worked example: one press travels from the pad, through the
quantiser, over the air to the inside box, and to the phone. The build runs fast
in the middle; the finished doorbell, the knob and the phone page close it.
First two seconds: IMG_7157 at the first press, pad lights, music starts on the
press, title "my doorbell has no button".
Beats: hook at the door → "this is what was in the wall" (old button, meter, two
wires) → "so the two wires carry 12 V and the music never leaves the box" (the
diagram grows: pad → engine → radio → inside; real clips as the nodes) → build
on the beat (coupon, plate, buttons, solder time-lapse, LEDs, link test) →
"5 boards. 1 survived." (one bar) → install (breaker, module, box on the wall)
→ PAYOFF: full performance at the door and the inside box answering → the orange
box, the knob, the phone page: quiet hours and the notification popping up →
claim "a doorbell you play, not press" → end card with the repo.
Look: footage as shot, graded warm; lime #c8ff2e as the one accent; white
condensed display type; the diagram drawn in the three row colours.
Sound: the track from the first press; door audio under the payoff; one-shots as
cut hits; chime on the end card.

## B. "Five boards died so this doorbell could live"
Device: a number lands. Form: object-to-object chain.
Length 60-75 s. A maker's confession. Each dead ESP32-S3 is a chapter: the
board in hand, its four-word cause (trimmer the wrong way; strip on 3V3; 12 V on
a pin; closed the lid on bare pins), a red stamp, and the next board being
unboxed. The fifth goes on the wall and plays. The result arrives late and hits
harder for it. Less about the doorbell, more about the lessons; strong with
makers, weaker with everyone else.
First two seconds: the S3 in hand, title "I killed 5 of these for a doorbell".
Beats: boards 1-4 with lessons → board 5 flashed bare → piece by piece on the
bench → install → performance → end card.
Look: bench footage, tape and smoke; red for each death, lime for the survivor.
Sound: a muted track that only opens up when board 5 plays.

## C. "Door and kitchen, same beat"  (the bold one)
Device: the feature as the hook. Form: a split frame that is the story.
Length 60-80 s. Left half: the door. Right half: the inside box. The visitor
plays, and both halves flash and sound together, 30 ms apart; the sync IS the
film. The build appears as short inserts between performances, and the ending
widens to the phone receiving the notification as a third panel. Needs one new
recording: two phones filming at once, one at the door, one at the inside box,
while someone plays (the two existing clips are different performances).
First two seconds: both halves at the same moment, finger about to press,
title "one press, two rooms".
Beats: split hook → "what crosses the door is 30 bytes" → inserts: wall, build,
install → split performance → three panels with the phone → end card.
Look: hard vertical split, lime centre line that pulses on the beat.
Sound: door audio left, inside audio right, the track under both.

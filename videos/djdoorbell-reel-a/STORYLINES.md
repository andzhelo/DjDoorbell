# DJ Doorbell reel — three storylines (2026-10-31)

Vertical 1080x1920, 28-34 s, music = the doorbell's own 124 BPM track
(data/preview/doorbell-house-demo.wav), no voiceover, English titles, cuts on the 16th grid.

## A. "My doorbell is a drum machine"  (RECOMMENDED)
Device: the result first. Form: cut on the beat.
A hand slams the pads at the door in frame 0, the lights flash and the first press
IS the first kick of the music. Then the film rewinds through how it got there, every
cut on the beat, and lands back at the door with the phone.
First two seconds: IMG_7119 at the moment the hand hits pad 7 (amber flash), title
"my doorbell is a drum machine" already on screen, music starts on the press.
Beats: hook at the door → "it started with this" (old Nisko button, 8 V meter) →
"so I ordered everything" (parts bags spread, S3 in hand, strip, buck: 4 shots in one bar)
→ build on the beat (coupon, lime faceplate print, buttons in, solder time-lapse, LEDs first light,
link test through the door) → "5 boards. 1 survived." (smoked board, tape wrap) →
install (breaker, IRM-10-12, box on the wall) → PAYOFF: full-volume performance at the door,
inside unit answers → phone pad in hand, tap, door lights → end card "DJ Doorbell · github.com/andzhelo/DjDoorbell".
Look: the footage as it is, graded warm; lime #c8ff2e of the printed box as the only accent, on type and
on a 16-step grid strip that pulses with the music; condensed heavy display type, white.
Sound: the project's own track from the first kick; the actual door speaker audio under the payoff; chime at the end.

## B. "Every visitor becomes a DJ"
Device: a moment in someone's day. Form: one continuous situation.
Someone arrives at the door, finds nine buttons instead of a bell, hesitates, presses one, the beat
starts and they keep playing; inside the apartment the chime unit plays the same performance.
Then a fast "how" montage and the phone. Needs one new shot of a stranger / friend at the door
reacting (we only have the owner's hand). Strong story, weakest footage fit today.
First two seconds: a finger hovering over the pad, title "there is no doorbell button".
Beats: hesitation → first press → performance → inside speaker → 10-second how → end card.
Look: hallway footage, lime accent, same type. Sound: silence until the first press, then the track.

## C. "I killed 5 ESP32 boards to build a doorbell"
Device: a number lands. Form: object-to-object chain.
A maker's confession: each dead board is a beat with its cause in four words (trimmer the wrong way,
strip on 3V3, 12 V on a pin, closed the lid), each ending on the next board being unboxed, until the
fifth one goes on the wall and plays. The result arrives late and hits harder for it.
First two seconds: the S3 board in hand, title "I killed 5 of these for a doorbell".
Beats: boards 1-4 and their lessons → board 5 flashed → install → performance at the door → phone → end card.
Look: bench footage, tape and smoke; red accent for each death, lime for the one that lived.
Sound: a dry click per dead board, the track only when board 5 plays.
